About Our Firm & Leadership

Headquartered at Meydan Grandstand in Dubai, TruePath is an exclusive B2B talent acquisition, executive search, and HR consultancy partnering with organizations across the UAE and the wider GCC.

Our Corporate Mission

Strategic Talent Acquisition Built on Trust, Precision, and Discretion

TruePath operates exclusively on a B2B model. We do not run public resume dropboxes or push uncalibrated CVs. Instead, our practice partners actively map and engage the top tier of executive and specialized talent across 12 core industry sectors in Dubai and international corridors.

From retained C-suite headhunting and permanent placements to turnkey mass staffing, UAE government relations (PRO/GRO), and managed HR operations, we ensure your organization scales with velocity and total regulatory peace of mind.
